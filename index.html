Overview

FAN-MPC coordinates stability and energy-efficient torque allocation on continuous curved roads with varying adhesion. The upper layer generates nominal torque references inside a physically admissible motor and tire-force region. Offline Bayesian optimization supplies feasible near-optimal weight labels. Five target-specific RBF neural predictors schedule bounded, smoothed MPC priorities from current and preview road conditions. The constrained lower-layer LTV-MPC—not the neural network—computes the final four-wheel torque commands.
